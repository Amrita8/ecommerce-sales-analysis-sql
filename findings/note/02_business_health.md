## 1. Revenue Trajectory & Monthly Trends
Top-line growth surged in April 2026, peaking at **104.25M**—an **+82.2% month-over-month increase** driven by a volume surge to 16,352 orders. However, performance contracted significantly in May (-40.9%) and dropped sharply in June down to **16.45M** (2,643 orders). 

## 2. AOV vs. Volume Dynamics
Deconstructing the growth drivers reveals that total revenue movements are **100% volume-driven**:
* **Stable Ticket Size:** AOV varies by less than 2.5% across all four months (ranging from 6,223.48 in June to 6,375.60 in April), indicating consistent basket size and item pricing.
* **Volume Instability:** Order volume spiked by +79.5% from March to April before collapsing by -83.8% from April to June. 

## 3. One-Line Board Answer & Recommendations
* **Are we growing?** **We are currently contracting sharply following an April peak, with overall revenue dependent entirely on order acquisition volume rather than expanding cart values.**

**Strategic Focus for the Board:**
1. **Investigate the April Demand Surge:** Determine whether April's revenue peak was tied to a specific promotional campaign, inventory release, or marketing spend that can be replicated.
2. **Address the June Volume Drop:** Investigate potential data truncation in June or underlying retention issues causing the steep drop off in order volume.
