# Business Overview & Data Exploration Summary

**To:** Chief Technology Officer  
**From:** Amrita Saha Gupta, Data Analyst  

## 1. Business & Model Overview
Our business operates as an e-commerce platform offering a multi-category product catalog to a geographically distributed customer base. Transactional records indicate a retail model driven primarily by consumer electronics, personal audio equipment, and wearable technology (such as smartwatches, active noise-canceling headphones, and wireless earbuds).

## 2. Platform Scale & Timeline
* **Customer Base:** 8800 unique registered customers.
* **Order Volume:** 40000 completed transaction orders.
* **Operational Timeline:** Data spans from 2026-03-16 to 2026-06-14.
* **Top Grossing Product:** **Vastra Craft Pulse Kids Smartwatch** leading single-product revenue at **1,411,795.38** (49 units sold).
* **Highest Volume Product:** **Zyra Bass+ Over-Ear Headphones** generating the highest sales volume with **57 units** sold (1,298,399.44 total revenue).

## 3. Key Observations & Surprises
While analyzing product performance and customer purchasing habits, a few distinct patterns surfaced:

* **Kids' Wearables Demand:** Kids' smartwatches represent an unexpectedly large share of top revenue, accounting for 3 of the top 10 highest-grossing individual products.
* **High Average Unit Value:** Across the top 10 revenue-generating products, unit prices consistently range between ~22,000 and ~41,000, indicating a premium positioning rather than a low-cost volume model.
